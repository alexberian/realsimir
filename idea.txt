the goal:
generate realistic IR images from unlabeled synthetic IR images of ships. We also have real IR data of ships, sometimes the real mages have many ships.

the idea:
start with a foundation diffusion pretrained model, such as edm2. re train on the unlabeled real data in an unsupervised manner. to make a training image, we generate a bounding box of the ship using some bounding box algorithm. jitter the box by making it 10 or 20% larget than the ship. paste it back into the image slightly offset or rotated pixel gain changed or noised or whatever else. crop out a 256x256 image in the same spot from the doctored and original image. then we train the model to output the clean image. in this way the model is learning to clean poorly cropped images. then we can take croppings of the synthetic IR ship images and paste them into realworld images, then ask the model to do what it does best, clean poor-looking croppings. we train the diffusion model with noised versions of the target image of varying noise levels.
